FOUR FREE WINDOWS APPS
======================
Nothing to install. Unzip this folder anywhere (Desktop, Documents, a USB stick), then double-click an app.
Each one opens in its own window using Microsoft Edge, which comes with Windows 10 and 11.
Closing the window closes the app.

FIRST TIME YOU OPEN ONE
Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because these apps aren't from the Microsoft Store.
Click "More info", then "Run anyway". You only have to do this once per app.


1. MarketTerminal.exe  (ready to use, no sign-up)
   Four apps in one; open the other three from the buttons along the top:
   - Market Terminal: stocks, bonds, currencies, commodities, the economy and market news.
     Type a ticker such as AAPL to see a company's charts, financials, analyst ratings and more.
   - Portfolio Lab: test how a mix of investments would have done in the past.
   - Signal Monitor: see when a stock is suddenly getting attention online.
   - Influence Watch: who is pushing which story in the news, foreign-agent and lobbying records,
     stock trades by members of Congress, company insiders' trades, and federal contracts.
     (Optional: to see insiders' buy and sell amounts, add your email under HOW IT WORKS > Settings.
     It is only sent to the SEC, which asks apps for a contact address.)

2. BusinessClassFinder.exe  (needs a free SerpApi key)
   Business-class fares from Washington DC, New York and Chicago to Europe and Asia, with hotels and
   whether to pay with cash or points.
   Setup: sign up at https://serpapi.com/users/sign_up, copy your key from
   https://serpapi.com/manage-api-key, and paste it into the app. The free plan includes a limited number
   of searches each month; the app shows how many you have left.

3. UsedCarFinder.exe  (needs a free MarketCheck key)
   Search used cars by type, years and mileage (nationwide or near your ZIP) and see which ones are
   priced below similar cars, plus recalls and owner complaints.
   Setup: get a free key at https://www.marketcheck.com/sign-up and paste it into the app.

4. LastMinuteTickets.exe  (needs a free SeatGeek and/or Ticketmaster key)
   Games, concerts, shows and comedy near you over the next few days: the cheapest seat right now,
   face value, price drops, and price alerts, with buttons for Gametime, SeatGeek and Ticketmaster.
   Starts on Washington, DC; change the city or ZIP code in Settings.
   Setup (one or both, a couple of minutes each):
   - SeatGeek: https://seatgeek.com/account/develop > create an app > copy the "Client ID".
   - Ticketmaster: https://developer-acct.ticketmaster.com/user/register > My Apps > copy the "Consumer Key".


YOUR DATA
Your keys, settings and saved searches stay on your own PC, in
C:\Users\<you>\AppData\Local\<app name>. They are only sent to the service each key belongs to.
To carry them on a USB stick instead, make an empty folder next to the app named:
  MarketTerminal-data / BusinessClassFinder-data / UsedCarFinder-data / LastMinuteTickets-data
Anyone who has that folder can use your keys, so don't share it.

The apps use free public data, may be wrong or out of date, and are not financial advice.
Always check prices on the airline, dealer or ticket site before buying.
